DATA SOURCES AND TERMS
======================

Land prices
-----------
Formal name: 国土数値情報（地価公示）（令和8年）
Provider: Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT), Japan
Official data page: https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/gml/datalist/KsjTmplt-L01-2026.html
Terms: The official page labels 2019 onward as open data (CC BY 4.0). The bundled
2026 data is derived from the 2026 edition, as of January 1, 2026. Source data
fields were selected, converted into local JSON, and arranged into annual price
history for this application.
Required attribution displayed by this site:
「国土数値情報（地価公示）（令和8年）」（国土交通省）を加工して作成
Official terms: https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/other/agreement.html
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Prefecture boundaries
---------------------
Formal name: Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 – States, Provinces, version 5.1.1
Provider: Natural Earth
Official source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/
Terms: Natural Earth's official terms place its vector and raster data in the
public domain; permission and credit are not required. This site includes the
recommended credit “Made with Natural Earth.”
Terms page: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Transformation: the Japan/admin=Japan records were selected from the official
source Shapefile, retaining the 47 Japanese prefecture polygons and Japanese
prefecture names; converted to EPSG:4326 GeoJSON without geometry simplification.
This is a cartographic first-order boundary dataset, not a survey/legal boundary
product. No Geospatial Information Authority of Japan boundary data is included.
